## 7. A. Safe rejections vs B. unnecessary rejections
**A (successful fail-closed on unsafe/unverifiable frames).** V0.1 emitted 240 frames in known/inferred-bad sets (wrong person, merged skeletons, distorted pose); V0.2 emits 33 of them (14%), turns 202 UNRESOLVED and 5 PREDICTED. 18 labelled wrong-person frames: 0 re-emitted on V0.1's wrong box (f617/618 are on a different box, viewed as Cena). Viewed contaminated frames: 16 → 4 still emitted (f270, 925, 950, 1075). **A is real but incomplete.**

**B (unnecessary rejection).** Viewed-clean frames lost: 9 of 21 (f25, 50, 100, 325, 700, 725, 750, 755, 825). Fresh sample of 22 V0.2-UNRESOLVED frames that V0.1 tracked and that are in no known-bad set (stratified by reason; `scratch/adjud_sample.json`; one non-blinded reviewer): **4 justified** (f81 adjacent bodies, f125 ambiguity, f143 merged/lifted, f325 opponent inside box, uncertain) and **18 judged unnecessary** (Cena visible and single-bodied) = 82% (Wilson 95% CI 61-93%; stratified, tiny, non-blinded). Causes of the 18: spurious overlap from low-confidence neighbouring detections or detections whose boxes do not touch Cena (f66, 209, 824, 829, 831, 833) ×6; identity-cue limits (legs/shorts not usable: f705, 729, 730, 732, 810, 818) ×6; probation (f157, 204, 329, 739) ×4 (157 is contact-heavy); ambiguity/integrity false alarms on a clearly isolated bent-over Cena (f640, 644) ×2. "Unnecessary" means Cena was visibly measurable; it does **not** prove the COM would have been correct. Rough extrapolation to the 199-frame pool: most (~75-90%) of V0.1's not-known-bad frames that V0.2 dropped were probably valid measurement opportunities. The 33 retained known-bad frames and the fresh suspects (f224, 273, 767, 1082) show A is incomplete.

## 8. New failure modes observed (not in V0.1)
1. **Spurious overlap rejection** from low-confidence ghost or non-touching detections (6 of 22 sampled).
2. **Probation cost** after every cut and on first contact.
3. **Misleading reason code** COLOUR_CUE_INVALID_PHOTOMETRIC for scene-colour non-specificity (f376-392).
4. **Single-point protection** at f753 (see §2).
5. **Thigh-evidence dependency**: 551 frames have no hip/knee keypoints; plainly visible torso close-ups cannot be identified.
6. **Integrity/ambiguity false alarms** on isolated bent-over poses (f640, 644).
7. **Residual contamination** passing every guard (4 of 16 viewed + ~4 suspected in a 24-frame fresh sample).
Not observed: any V0.2 emission on a clearly wrong person in the fresh 24-frame sample; any off-image COM.

## 9. Hard-coding / frame-specific exceptions
None. `grep` of `track_v02.py`, `integrity.py`, `cuts_v02.py`, `config_v02.json` finds no frame-number literals (matches were colour/255 values). The only frame constant is the manual identity seed (FRAME 99, detection 0), inherited from V0.1. **But the design was informed by benchmark frames:** I examined thigh-region coverage and background-colour fractions at f387/392/615/753 and cut-event statistics for all 28 events before fixing thresholds, and removed shoulder-separation checks after run 1. Thresholds are in `config_v02.json`; none was swept against benchmark results, but benchmark results are in-sample.

## 10. Conclusion
A: V0.2 rejects most known-unsafe V0.1 emissions (202 of 240) and eliminates off-image predictions and the four false cuts. B: it does so at a large cost: probably most of the ~200 non-flagged V0.1 frames it dropped were measurable, and it still emits some contaminated frames. **V0.2 is a fail-closed feasibility prototype, not a validated measurement system.** No tuning has been done in response to this adjudication.
