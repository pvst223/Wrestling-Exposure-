# V0.2 architecture and rationale

**Design rule: FAIL CLOSED.** A COM is emitted only if identity, single-body integrity and probation all pass. Otherwise the frame is UNRESOLVED with a machine-readable reason. A correctly UNRESOLVED frame is preferable to a confidently wrong one.

**V0.1 is untouched** (all V0.1 hashes re-verified; see REPRODUCIBILITY.md). V0.2 lives in `v02/`, reuses V0.1's *cached pose detections* read-only (`out/raw_detections.pkl`; the YOLO stage is unchanged and was not re-run) and a byte-identical copy of the COM model (`src/com_model.py`, sha256 7f4d86fc…, same as V0.1). **The conceptual target is unchanged: one estimated whole-body COM from multi-landmark Dempster segments, renormalised over visible mass. It is still NOT validated against any external reference.**

## Pipeline
cached detections + video → camera-event classifier (`cuts_v02.py`) → per-frame colour-cue validity → candidates (thigh-region colour evidence + continuity gate + ambiguity check + probation) → single-body integrity & overlap context (`integrity.py`) → COM / status (same segment model and mass thresholds as V0.1) → bounded prediction → CSV, raw landmarks, annotated video. Everything tunable is in `src/config_v02.json`.

## Architectural changes, each tied to a confirmed V0.1 failure mechanism

| # | V0.1 mechanism (confirmed) | V0.2 change | Rationale | Honest limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity = denim fraction in lower 65% of the *box*; any blue region (mat at f387-392, flash at f753) passes | Colour evidence is measured only inside **thigh regions built from the candidate's own hip/knee keypoints**, must cover ≥0.35 of them (seed Cena = 0.57), region ≥600 px | Shorts are on the thighs; mat/banner pixels in the box are not. | Needs hip+knee keypoints; no thigh region ⇒ no identity (fail closed). Colour is still the principal cue; nothing here is re-identification. |
| 2 | Same cue fooled by global colour shifts (f292, 473, 628, 753, 1178) | **Colour-specificity guard:** if >15% of non-person pixels match the denim rule, or the frame is a classified transient event, the cue is invalid → UNRESOLVED (`COLOUR_CUE_INVALID_PHOTOMETRIC`) | A cue that matches the background is not evidence. | 56 frames lost, including f386-392 where Cena's close-up scene is itself blue-ish. |
| 3 | Continuity-only acceptance; continuity reset at every cut; no motion veto | No continuity-only acceptance. Continuity (IoU/displacement/scale gate, ≤5-frame gap) is a **veto** and a score bonus; ambiguity between two passing candidates ⇒ UNRESOLVED; **probation**: 3 consecutive same-lineage identity passes before any COM | Fail closed on first contact; a single-frame colour hit cannot create a measurement. | Costs ~25 probation frames and slows acquisition after cuts (median first emission per shot 3.5 frames vs 0). |
| 4 | Selected skeleton could contain other bodies (pose contamination), no in-run check | `integrity.py`: robust body scale; limb-length upper bounds; torso-side symmetry; landmarks inside own box; temporal scale/joint jump; **overlap context** = any other raw detection (≥0.10 conf) overlapping the box or with ≥3 joints inside it (near-duplicates excluded) ⇒ `OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED`, not emitted | Merged bodies require a second body nearby; fail closed whenever one is. | Geometric plausibility cannot prove single-body. If the merged skeleton is plausible and no second detection exists, it passes: **4 of 16 viewed contaminated frames still pass (f270, 925, 950, 1075).** Overlap context also rejects correct frames. |
| 5 | Cut detector treats "background does not register" as a cut ⇒ 4 false cuts | Same candidate stage, plus **persistence**: a cut starts a new *stable* shot that does not return to the old scene. Unstable after the event (>0.40 histogram distance within 8 frames) or scene returns (≤0.25 within 12 frames) ⇒ not a cut | Matches the physical difference between a camera change and a flash/exposure event. | **In-sample**: thresholds were chosen after probing these 28 events (probe scripts in `scratch/`); single-reviewer labels; no other clip. Pairwise ORB and context ORB were tried and did NOT separate the cases. |
| 6 | Cut resets removed the evidence against a wrong identity (f753) | Identity evidence no longer depends on cut state (colour-specificity and thigh-region checks run every frame); a flash frame cannot be accepted whatever the cut classifier says | Safety must not depend on the least reliable component. | Continuity is still reset at confirmed cuts. |
| 7 | Prediction after insufficient frames, 15-frame horizon, off-image COMs, multi-frame velocity quirk | PREDICTED only from a MEASURED anchor, ≤6 frames, no cut, **no taint** (integrity/overlap/ambiguity/jump rejection since anchor cancels prediction), only when no candidate matched or anatomy was insufficient; velocity from consecutive emitted frames only; point must be inside the image **and** within 1 box-diagonal of the anchor box, else UNRESOLVED (`PREDICTION_OUT_OF_BOUNDS`). | Zero off-image predictions by construction. | Prediction after a frame with a selected-but-insufficient candidate **is allowed** (documented, identical to the behaviour the audit described, but now bounded). Predictions are extrapolations, never measurements. |
| 8 | `tracking_confidence` = det_conf × saturating identity × landmark conf; high on wrong people | `evidence_quality_ordinal` = **min** of identity evidence (coverage/seed, penalised without continuity), mean keypoint confidence, visible mass. MEASURED additionally requires identity+integrity+probation. | A weakest-link score cannot be raised by a confident pose model on the wrong body. | Still an **ordinal heuristic, not a probability and not calibrated**. |
| 9 | Behavioural literals scattered; docs/code drift | All behavioural constants in `config_v02.json`; `check_config_usage.py` proves none unused and lists remaining literals (drawing only); component docs are generated from the same names | Auditability. | Keypoint index tables and drawing colours remain in code. |

## Design errors found in my first V0.2 run (disclosed)
Run 1 (archived in `out/run1_archive/`) included shoulder/hip lateral-separation checks (`SHOULDERS_COINCIDENT`). They wrongly rejected clean profile/back views (f50, 100, 625), contradicting the foreshortening principle I had stated. I removed them (and two unused config keys) for **run 2**, which is the reported V0.2. No other change was made after seeing results; the benchmark was not edited and no frame-specific exceptions exist. Run-1 vs run-2 effect: viewed clean frames retained 11→12 of 21 (f625 recovered; f50/100 are still lost, now to OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED); MEASURED+PARTIAL 283→330.

## What V0.2 does not do (needs ground truth that does not exist)
A learned re-identification model, anatomical landmark accuracy, COM validity, multi-body detection beyond geometric plausibility and neighbour detections, identity in close-ups without visible shorts, two-wrestler tracking. These are not claimed.
