# V0.1 vs V0.2 comparison on the same clip and the frozen pre-development reference benchmark

## Read this first: what the benchmark is
No independent ground-truth benchmark existed in the workspace when V0.2 began. `benchmark/benchmark_v0.1_reference.json` (sha256 e27aa114…, read-only, never edited) was assembled **only from labels already written before any V0.2 code**: the independent auditor's Pass-2 findings (6 wrong-person frames, cut review) and my own single-reviewer, non-blinded spot-check verdicts (~75 viewed frames). It has **no landmark or COM ground truth**, is small, is partly developer-made, and **contains the frames that motivated V0.2: every number below is in-sample and says nothing about other clips.** I viewed some frame statistics of those frames while designing V0.2 (thigh-region coverage at f387/392/615/753, background-colour fraction, cut-event probes); thresholds were set from stated principles, not by iterating results, with the single disclosed exception (run 1 → run 2).

**Missingness tradeoff:** of V0.1's 752 emitted frames, 240 lie in known/inferred-bad sets and V0.2 keeps 33 of them (14%); 512 are not known to be bad and V0.2 keeps 297 (58%), dropping 199 (reasons: overlap-unverified 106, identity not matched 67, probation 18, integrity 5, ambiguity 2, colour cue 1). "Not known to be bad" is not "known good". 5+16 further V0.1-emitted frames became PREDICTED.

## Camera events (in-sample)
V0.1 24 cuts (20 true, 4 false: 292, 434, 753, 1062; precision 83.3%). V0.2 20 cuts, 20 true, 0 false, 0 missed (precision/recall 100%/100% **in-sample**, single reviewer). The four false events are classified TRANSIENT_UNSTABLE_AFTER (292, 753, 1062) or SAME_SCENE_RETURNS (434); f473, 491, 628, 1178 stay PHOTOMETRIC_REGISTERED. Shots 25 → 21. No cut was missed on this clip; a missed real cut on another clip is the main new risk (hist-distance gap between true cuts and false ones is narrow: 0.25 vs 0.27-0.28 at f1397).

## Prediction behaviour
V0.1: 76 PREDICTED, **18 off-image** (f478-488, 657-659, 661-662, 759, 762), up to 15 frames, resumed after insufficient frames. V0.2: 28 PREDICTED, **0 off-image** (enforced), max 6 frames since a MEASURED anchor, none after integrity/overlap/ambiguity rejection. 8 V0.2 predictions follow a frame with a selected candidate (allowed when anatomy was insufficient or the candidate failed appearance; documented).

## Reacquisition (emission-based, not identity accuracy)
V0.1: 18 of 25 shots acquired (median latency 0, max 25). V0.2: 12 of 21 shots have at least one emission; median first-emission latency 3.5 frames, max 32; 65 loss runs after first emission, 56 reacquired.

## Not established
Anatomical/COM validity; accuracy of any emitted frame (none of the 330 is verified); generalisation (any other clip, wrestler, camera); that the remaining 297 "unflagged" retained frames are correct; whether the thresholds are good outside this clip; recall of contamination detection beyond the 16 viewed frames (12 of 16 caught). The auditor and I are single reviewers; neither is a trained biomechanist.
