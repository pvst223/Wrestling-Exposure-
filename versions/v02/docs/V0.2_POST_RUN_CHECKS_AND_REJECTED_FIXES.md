# Post-run checks and fixes I tried and did NOT ship (V0.2 run 2 is unchanged)

Requested: "tweak whatever was off". After re-checking, the remaining misses cannot be fixed by a defensible tweak, so **V0.2 run 2 is the audit candidate, unmodified** (hashes in `out/V0.2_SHA256SUMS.txt` are unchanged). The attached V0.1 video was re-hashed: it is byte-identical to the frozen baseline (sha256 3631c547…).

## 1. Fresh-sample visual check (24 emitted frames, random, none from the benchmark; seed 20261014; `scratch/fresh_sample_frames.json`)
Single non-blinded reviewer (me). No clear wrong-person emission seen. About 4 of 24 emitted frames look suspect for merged or contaminated skeletons or are uncertain: f224 and f273 (carry/lift), f767 (prone grapple), f1082 (grapple); f158 (Cena on top of opponent) is plausible but contact-heavy. Together with the 4 of 16 benchmark frames still failing (f270, 925, 950, 1075), **residual contamination in V0.2 emissions is real and likely 15–25% of emitted frames in contact sequences; this estimate comes from tiny samples and is not a rate.**

## 2. Why the residual contamination is not detectable with current signals
In f270 and f1075 no other person detection exists near the box at all; in f925/950 the nearby detections are not overlapping it (IoU≈0, ≤2 joints inside). The detector returned one merged box, so neighbour-based checks cannot fire.

## 3. Fixes tried and rejected (all evaluated offline, nothing shipped)
| Idea | Result | Why rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Simple geometry ratios (box height / leg length, torso / thigh, head-to-shoulder) | Contaminated and clean frames overlap (e.g. box-height/leg 2.26–2.45 contaminated vs 1.75–2.54 clean) | No separation |
| Lower the overlap thresholds | Other bodies have 0 joints inside / IoU 0 in the 4 failing frames | Cannot trigger without also rejecting most clean frames |
| Merge memory: a person seen separately in the last 15 frames now covered by the selected box and no longer detected | Caught f925 and f950 but flagged 112 of 330 emitted frames, including 7 of the 12 retained viewed-clean frames (bystanders walking behind) | Cost far exceeds benefit; would be fit to 2 frames |
| Recency of a previous overlap/integrity rejection | Clean frames are as close to rejections as contaminated ones | No separation |
| Re-running the pose model on an upscaled crop of the selected box to expose a second body | Merged detections stay a single person; second detections tiny (area fraction ≤0.08) in all contaminated frames; none in f270, f1075 | Does not separate bodies |

Any rule that catches the 4 remaining frames would be fitted to 4 benchmark frames and would not be evidence of generalisation. Realistic options need new information: an instance-segmentation or multi-person model that separates touching bodies, independently labelled single-body frames, or a re-identification model. These are V0.3-scale decisions, not tweaks.

## 4. What the auditor should therefore expect
V0.2 run 2 will still emit COMs on some multi-body frames. The audit should quantify how many on a **fresh independent sample of emitted frames** (not the benchmark: the benchmark frames were used to design V0.2 and are burned for generalisation claims).
