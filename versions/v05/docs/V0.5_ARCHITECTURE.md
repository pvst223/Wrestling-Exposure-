# V0.5 architecture (relative to V0.4)

Development prototype. Nothing is validated; every constant is a provisional convention; all results are in-sample on one clip.

## Four separate questions (never collapsed)
1. **Identity evidence:** is this detection wrestler A or B? Independent appearance evidence only (MobileCLIP-B box/upper/lower/masked-box embeddings vs 10 manual seeds + HSV colour regions + denim thigh cue). Continuity and pose consistency are logged separately and are NOT identity verification. Joint A/B assignment, anti-identity margin, collapse check and reacquisition rules as V0.4, except D8.
2. **Landmark ownership evidence:** do the landmarks that feed the COM lie in one person's silhouette that no other person's region claims? (`ownership_v05.py`, mask-based; geometry/temporal/appearance vetoes added in `integrity_v05.py` and `track_v05.py`.)
3. **Anatomical plausibility:** limb lengths, torso symmetry, landmark-in-own-box, body scale, temporal scale jump. Plain failures only.
4. **COM eligibility:** requires 1 (independent) AND 2 (SINGLE_BODY_SUPPORTED) AND 3 (PASS) AND probation AND visible mass >= 0.4.

## Ownership layer
Segmentation: YOLOv8m-seg (COCO 'person'), imgsz 960, conf 0.10, masks cached at 320x180; instances below `SEG_CONF_MIN` 0.25 ignored. Owner mask = the instance containing the largest fraction of the skeleton's confident landmarks (>= 0.6 required). Landmarks (nose, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles with conf >= 0.5) are classed OWN_ONLY / CONTESTED / OTHER_ONLY / OUTSIDE (mask dilated by 4 px). Further cues: other detected people's landmarks inside the owner mask; unexplained mask share (owner-mask pixels farther than 0.6 x body scale from every skeleton bone/keypoint); bones leaving the mask; bones spanning two instances.
States: **SINGLE_BODY_SUPPORTED** (own-only >= 0.8, contested <= 0.1, no other-instance landmarks, no opponent landmarks inside, unexplained <= 0.25, <= 1 bone leaving the mask; limits x0.5 under merge risk), **MIXED_BODY_SUSPECTED** (a bone spans two instances; >= 2 landmarks (>= 20%) belong exclusively to another instance; or any cross-body cue: limb too long, torso asymmetric, shared-joint conflict, part-identity conflict, temporal joint jump, colour-region conflict), **OWNERSHIP_UNCERTAIN** (everything else that fails SINGLE: no owner mask, contested landmarks, opponent landmarks in the mask, unexplained mask, too few own-only landmarks, bones leaving the mask).
**Establishes:** the landmarks lie in one segmented region, no other segmented region or detected person claims them, and that region is roughly explained by this skeleton.
**Does not establish:** that the instance mask is correct; that a joint inside a single merged mask belongs to the claimed person; that landmark positions are right; identity. Two people merged into one mask whose area is still "explained" by one skeleton (e.g. one body draped over the other) can pass; the vetoes (geometry, temporal jump, part-identity conflict) are the only remaining defences and are weak.
Appearance is never positive ownership evidence: it can only veto.

## Status vocabulary
MEASURED, PARTIAL, PREDICTED, UNRESOLVED (definitions in `V0.5_STATUS_AND_CONFIDENCE_DEFINITIONS.md`).

## Prediction
Only from a MEASURED anchor, <= 6 frames, no taint, reason in {NO_CANDIDATE, IDENTITY_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, IDENTITY_LOW_SCORE, LOW_ANATOMY_MASS}, inside the image and within one anchor-box diagonal, heuristic uncertainty radius (0.1 + 0.1k) x anchor-box diagonal <= 0.5 diagonal. IDENTITY_CONTRA_CUE, ownership states, collapse and ambiguity terminate prediction. Camera cuts reset spatial continuity (unchanged `cuts_events.py`).

## COM
Unchanged `com_model.py` (Dempster/Winter, 2D pixels). Each emitted row lists the segments and landmarks that contributed and how many of them are OWN_ONLY / CONTESTED / OUTSIDE.

`tracking_v05.csv`, `tracking_v05_landmarks_raw.csv` (per landmark ownership class and used_for_com), `candidates_v05.csv`, `identity_ownership_events_v05.csv`, `prediction_log_v05.csv`, `annotated_v05.mp4`, `run_meta_v05.json`, `cuts_v05.json`, comparison/regression files (post-freeze).
