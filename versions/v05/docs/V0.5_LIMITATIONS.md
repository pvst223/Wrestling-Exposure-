# V0.5 limitations (stated before the frozen run; results-dependent items are added in the self-audit)

Development prototype. In-sample on one 1,501-frame clip that informed the design of every version. No ground truth for identity, landmarks, masks or COM. All adjudication is single-reviewer and not independent. Nothing is validated.

## Ownership evidence
1. **The masks are not validated.** YOLOv8m-seg is a COCO model, never evaluated on this footage. Instance masks of touching people can merge, split arbitrarily or leak across people.
2. **Merged-mask passes.** Two bodies in one mask that one skeleton roughly "explains" (a body draped over another; a carried wrestler inside the carrier's outline) can still be SINGLE_BODY_SUPPORTED. Only geometry/temporal/appearance vetoes remain and they are weak. This class (carry scenes) is the central residual risk.
3. **Within-mask mixing.** A joint that lies inside a single mask but belongs to the other person cannot be told apart by anything in V0.5.
4. **Mask alignment.** Masks come from letterboxed network output resized to 320x180; edges may be off by up to about 2 px (checked on one frame). Tolerance 4 px absorbs most of this, and also makes CONTESTED/OTHER_ONLY less sensitive at boundaries.
5. **Constants.** All ownership constants are a priori conventions; only UNEXPL_DIST_S was set from the 9 usable clean seed detections (rule in changelog D12). None is calibrated against ground truth; none is a probability.
6. **Fail-closed cost.** Landmarks that are legitimately outside masks (hands, feet) or poses that are bent/grounded/airborne can push a clean frame to OWNERSHIP_UNCERTAIN. Coverage is expected to fall.

## Identity evidence
7. MobileCLIP-B is a general image embedding, not person-ReID; cosine similarity is uncalibrated; reliability comes from 10 seed crops; behaviour on this clip is in-sample. The masked-box cue shares the same encoder and therefore the same weaknesses; it is not independent of the box cue's failure modes.
8. Seeds were chosen by me by eye (A = Cena, B = opponent, referee = NEITHER).

## COM and prediction
9. COM is a 2D pixel-space heuristic (Dempster/Winter lumped segments, COCO landmarks); a PARTIAL COM is biased toward visible segments by an unknown amount. Not validated against any reference.
10. Prediction is a damped constant-velocity extrapolation with a heuristic uncertainty radius, not a statistical interval.

## Process
11. V0.5 was developed after seeing V0.4's audits, adjudications and the regression frame list. Pre-registered holdout-gate criteria and report items are developer-set screening criteria. The clip cannot be used as an independent test of V0.5.
12. No holdout footage was used. A single run of a frozen system is one run, not a variance estimate.
