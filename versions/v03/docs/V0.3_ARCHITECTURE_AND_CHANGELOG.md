# V0.3 architecture and change log (relative to V0.2)

Status: **FROZEN** after the single full run on 2026-10-14 (code/config/seed hashes in `PRE_RUN_HASHES.txt` were re-verified unchanged after the run). V0.1 and V0.2 are untouched. Everything below is a research-prototype description. **Nothing here is validated** against ground truth for identity, landmarks or COM; constants are provisional conventions, not fitted or calibrated quantities. All results are on the development clip and are in-sample.

## 1. What V0.3 tries to do (and the V0.2 weaknesses it targets)
| V0.2 behaviour | V0.3 change |
|---|---|
| One identity cue: denim coverage of the target's own thigh polygons (single wrestler, single colour) | Several cues fused (whole-person colour, regional colour, optional denim, continuity, body scale), for **two** wrestlers A and B, plus a NEITHER class |
| Identity of one person among generic people | Relative evidence: a candidate must fit A better than B, better than NEITHER, and better than the other candidates |
| Any overlapping detection ⇒ OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED (reject) | Overlap alone does not reject. Rejection needs a contamination signal, or (when overlap / merge risk is present) the absence of positive coherence evidence |
| Colour cue invalid ⇒ frame lost | An invalid colour cue just has reliability 0; the other cues continue |
| Continuity only a veto and bonus | Continuity is one reliability-weighted cue, decays with frames since the last confirmed frame, is reset at cuts, and never refreshed by predictions |
| Status from landmark mass only | MEASURED additionally requires identity tier HIGH **and** anatomy tier VERIFIED; otherwise PARTIAL with explicit `status_limiting_factors` |
Unchanged from V0.2 (copied byte-identically, hashes in `V0.3_SHA256SUMS.txt`): camera-event classifier (`cuts_events.py` = V0.2 `cuts_v02.py`), COM model (`com_model.py` = V0.1/V0.2), pose-detection stage (`stage1_detect.py` = V0.1), prediction rule constants, status mass thresholds, geometric limb checks.

## 2. Inputs and the ONLY manual identity information
`seeds/seeds_v03_manual.json`: ten (frame, detection-index, identity) entries. I (the developer) identified the people by looking at the frames: A = John Cena, B = the opponent, NEITHER = referee. Frames **25, 99, 200, 550, 620** are seed frames and are excluded from every benchmark count. Frame 99/det 0 is the same seed V0.1/V0.2 used; the others are new manual information. No per-frame labels and no frame exceptions exist anywhere in the code. Seed polygons in non-upright poses (f99, f620, f550-B) are visibly imprecise (see `scratch/seeds_check.jpg`) and were not corrected.

## 3. Pipeline per frame
1. Candidates = pose detections with conf ≥ 0.3 and box height ≥ 50 px (as V0.2).
2. Appearance descriptors (colour histograms) for body regions placed by that candidate's own keypoints.
3. Cue supports/reliabilities for A and B (and NEITHER, appearance only) → fused scores.
4. Decision per wrestler with margins against other candidates and the competitor → identity accepted (tier HIGH/ACCEPT) or a reason code.
5. For an accepted candidate: anatomical verification → probation → COM → status.
6. Bounded prediction for frames without a measurement. 

## 4. Identity cues (all provisional)
Each cue returns a **support** in [-1,1] for each identity and a **reliability** in [0,1]. Fusion: `S_X = Σ r_k s_{k,X} / Σ r_k` (reliability-weighted mean, **equal cue weights**, no fitted weights); `R_X = Σ r_k`; `n` = number of cues with r ≥ 0.25.
* **Appearance (regional)**: HSV colour histogram (27 bins: dark, grey-mid, grey-light, 12 hue × 2 value) of torso / thigh / shank / upper-arm / head regions (polygons from the candidate's keypoints with conf ≥ 0.3; ≥ 120 px). Static models per identity from the seed frames. Distance = Bhattacharyya. **Scale references derived from the seeds themselves**: `d_sep` (A-vs-B model distance), `noise` (mean distance between two seed views of the same wrestler), `gap = d_sep − noise`; support = `clip(1 − 2(d − noise)/gap, −1, 1)`; a region type with `gap ≤ 0` or unknown noise has weight 0. Region weight = Dempster mass × pixel factor × min(1, gap/0.15).
* **Appearance (whole-person)**: histogram of the central 60% columns of the box (includes background); same scale logic; reliability capped at 0.5.
* **Denim (optional, A only)**: V0.2's thigh-polygon denim coverage, valid only if the background-denim guard passes (≤ 15%) and not a transient event. For B it counts as evidence *against* B only if the denim rule did not fire on B's seed thighs (checked at init; in this clip it did not fire, so it does).
* **Continuity**: distance/IoU/scale to that identity's last identity-confirmed box in the same shot; reliability `1 − gap/6`; zero after a cut or after 5 frames.
* **Body scale** (torso scale vs that identity's last confirmed scale): reliability capped at 0.5 × continuity reliability.
* **NEITHER**: appearance cues vs. referee exemplars only.

**Acceptance** for wrestler X on its best candidate c: `R ≥ 1.0`, `n ≥ 2`, no strong contra-cue (any cue with r ≥ 0.5 and s ≤ −0.8), `S ≥ 0.30`, margin to the next candidate ≥ 0.25, margin to the competitor identity (other wrestler or NEITHER) on the same candidate ≥ 0.25. Tier HIGH if additionally `S ≥ 0.5, R ≥ 2.0, n ≥ 3`. These numbers are conventions chosen a priori; none was tuned on benchmark results.

**Honest consequence observed:** with this seeding the seed-derived noise estimate says torso, shank, arm and head colour do **not** separate A from B beyond within-person variation (torso gap ≈ 0.01, shank −0.19, arm −0.05); only thigh colour (gap 0.41) and, weakly, whole-person colour (gap 0.08) do. The "regional appearance" cue therefore reduces mostly to thigh colour, which is the same information as the denim cue (not independent).

## 5. Two tracks and exclusivity
Separate state for A and B (last confirmed box/scale, probation lineage, previous emitted skeleton, prediction anchor, velocity, taint). A candidate that fits the other wrestler or NEITHER at least as well cannot be accepted (margin tests); one detection cannot be A and B at once (verified: 0 frames). **Not implemented**: a global assignment solver, or a constraint that A's and B's *skeletons* cannot be the same body (f1114: both emit, COMs 12 px apart).

## 6. Single-body anatomical verification (`integrity_v03.py`)
FAIL if any: no body scale; limb too long vs torso scale; torso sides asymmetric; landmark outside own box (10% margin); temporal scale jump; **torso-centred** joint displacement vs the identity's previous emitted skeleton (≥ 2 joints moving > 1 torso-scale × gap, gap ≤ 3); **appearance segment conflict** (regions voting for the *other* wrestler carry ≥ 0.15 of body mass; a region votes only if the colour-distance difference exceeds max(0.25·d_sep, noise)); **shared-joint conflict** (≥ 3 joints coincide within 5% of box diagonal with a non-duplicate detection).
Positive coherence = temporal check applicable and passed, **or** appearance own-identity votes ≥ 0.25 mass with no contra-votes.
Tier: VERIFIED (no FAIL and positive evidence) → may become MEASURED; GEOMETRY_ONLY (no FAIL, no positive evidence, no overlap/merge risk) → PARTIAL at most; if overlap context or merge risk exists and no positive evidence → OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED / MERGE_RISK_UNVERIFIED (not emitted). *Merge risk*: the other wrestler was confirmed within the last 10 frames, is not accepted now, and its last box centre lies inside this box (or IoU ≥ 0.3).
**Limits (see developer report §4):** every one of these is a plausibility test; temporal coherence is *self-referential* (a contaminated skeleton that persists is coherent with itself); appearance "own mass" can reach at most 0.2 here (only thighs separate the wrestlers), below the 0.25 required, so appearance never provided positive evidence in this run.

## 7. COM, status, confidence
COM definition and mass thresholds unchanged (`com_model.py`; not validated). MEASURED = mass ≥ 0.85 with trunk **and** identity tier HIGH **and** anatomy VERIFIED. PARTIAL = emitted but at least one of: LOW_MASS, IDENTITY_TIER_ACCEPT, ANATOMY_GEOMETRY_ONLY (recorded in `status_limiting_factors`). `evidence_quality_ordinal` = min(max(0,S), anatomy quality (1.0 verified / 0.6 geometry-only), mean keypoint confidence, min(1, mass)) — an ordinal score, **not a probability**, not calibrated.

## 8. Prediction
Only from a MEASURED anchor of the same wrestler, ≤ 6 frames, no taint since the anchor (ambiguity, anatomy failure, overlap/merge-unverified), point inside the image and within one anchor-box diagonal of the anchor box; reasons that allow prediction: NO_CANDIDATE, IDENTITY_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, IDENTITY_LOW_SCORE, IDENTITY_CONTRA_CUE, LOW_ANATOMY_MASS. Predictions never refresh continuity.

## 9. Reason codes (`unresolved_reason`)
NO_CANDIDATE, IDENTITY_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, IDENTITY_CONTRA_CUE, IDENTITY_LOW_SCORE, IDENTITY_AMBIGUOUS_CANDIDATES, IDENTITY_AMBIGUOUS_COMPETITOR, ANATOMY_FAIL (detail in `integrity_reasons`), OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED, MERGE_RISK_UNVERIFIED, PROBATION, LOW_ANATOMY_MASS, PREDICTION_OUT_OF_BOUNDS (0 occurrences).

## 10. Development history — disclosure
* One smoke test on frames 0–199 (crash check; it printed cue values on frames 99, 100, 150, 175 — **frames 100 and 175 are benchmark "clean target" frames**, so the development clip was looked at, not held out).
* **Iteration 1 (before the real run)**: the smoke test showed Cena's own torso scoring negative against his own seed model at the seed frame, because the original support scale (`1 − 2d/d_sep`) ignored within-person variation. Replaced by the seed-derived noise/gap scale above; votes made noise-aware; `IDENTITY_CONTRA_CUE` stopped marking a track as tainted (the best candidate being someone else is not evidence of track corruption).
* **Iteration 2 (before the real run)**: two seed views per wrestler gave an unusable noise estimate and a pessimistic max-based noise; seeds extended from 2 to 5 frames and noise changed from max to mean pairwise distance. Seed identities were verified visually (sheet kept in the package).
* No benchmark metric was consulted before the full run. After the run nothing was changed (hashes re-verified); a second run produced byte-identical outputs.
* No hard-coded frame exceptions exist in code. Frame numbers appear only in `seeds_v03_manual.json`.
