# V0.4 architecture (relative to V0.3)

Research prototype. **Nothing here is validated** against ground truth for identity, landmarks or COM; constants are provisional conventions fixed before the frozen run; all results are in-sample development performance on one clip. See `V0.4_REID_MODEL_SELECTION.md` for the embedding model and `V0.4_DEVELOPMENT_CHANGELOG.md` for every development decision.

## 1. Pipeline per frame
1. Candidates = pose detections with conf >= 0.3 and box height >= 50 px (as V0.2/V0.3).
2. Decomposed identity evidence per candidate (never collapsed into one hidden score):
   * **embedding evidence** (`reid_*`): MobileCLIP-B cosine similarity of BOX / UPPER / LOWER crops to the 10 seed exemplars; per-identity support from the margin vs the other wrestler and the referee ("is X" *and* "is not Y/NEITHER" in one number); reliability from seed leave-one-out accuracy x crop size. Uncalibrated.
   * **legacy colour evidence** (`colour_*`): V0.3's HSV regional/whole-person histograms and the optional denim-thigh cue, kept as a separate family.
   * **independent appearance evidence** (`ind_*`) = reliability-weighted mean of the embedding cues and the legacy colour cues (equal cue weights, no fitted weights).
   * **temporal/spatial continuity** (`continuity_*`) and **pose/body consistency** (`pose_consistency_*`): computed and logged separately; they are *not* part of `ind_*`.
   * **anti-identity margin** `g_X = S_ind(X) - max(S_ind(other wrestler), S_ind(NEITHER))` (the competitor terms only when reliable).
3. **Joint A/B assignment** (one decision for both wrestlers): all assignments of distinct candidates (or none) to A and B are scored by the sum of their `g`; each wrestler's *joint margin* is the best score minus the best score with a different choice for that wrestler. A wrestler gets an independent assignment only if the chosen candidate passes the evidence gates (R_ind >= 0.8, >= 2 present cues, no contra cue, S_ind >= 0.30, g >= 0.25) **and** the joint margin >= 0.25. Otherwise the wrestler is AMBIGUOUS/INSUFFICIENT/NOT_IN_VIEW with a reason code. Diagnostics logged: SAME_DETECTION_CONFLICT, AB_COLLAPSE_SUSPECTED, REACQUISITION, REACQUISITION_UNCERTAIN, CAMERA_CUT_SPATIAL_RESET (`identity_events_v04.csv`).
4. **Temporal continuation** (supporting evidence only): a wrestler lacking independent support (and only for reason INSUFFICIENT) may *continue* on the candidate that is spatially continuous with its last box, if its credit `0.85^(frames since last independent support)` >= 0.5, no candidate is claimed by the other wrestler, appearance does not contradict, the continuation is unique (margin 0.25), and there is no merge risk. State `TEMPORAL_CONTINUATION`; it can never produce MEASURED and it never refreshes the credit. Camera cuts reset spatial continuity, credit and anchors; static seed appearance models mean independent appearance evidence can still re-acquire across a cut.
5. **A/B collapse check** on the two selected skeletons (torso centres closer than 0.15 of the smaller box diagonal, or >= 3 shared joints): both become AMBIGUOUS (no assignment), both tainted.
6. **Reacquisition**: independent support after a loss/cut is logged; after ambiguity or collapse it additionally requires identity tier HIGH, else REACQUISITION_UNCERTAIN.
7. **Anatomical integrity** (`integrity_v04.py`) for the selected candidate, independent of identity: geometry (limb length, torso symmetry, landmark inside own box, scale jump), torso-centred temporal joint check, regional-colour contamination votes, shared-joint conflict (as V0.3), plus **PART_IDENTITY_CONFLICT** (the UPPER and LOWER embeddings of one skeleton disagree about who it is) and the independent positive evidence **APPEARANCE_PART_COHERENT** (>= 2 usable parts all support the assigned identity). Tiers: FAIL, UNVERIFIED (overlap/merge-risk context without *independent* positive evidence), PASS/INDEPENDENT, PASS/TEMPORAL_ONLY, PASS/GEOMETRY_ONLY. **Temporal coherence alone is never "verified"**; with overlap/merge risk it no longer rescues a frame (this reverses V0.3's overlap relaxation).
8. **COM eligibility** is a separate state: ELIGIBLE / INELIGIBLE (ANATOMY_FAIL, OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED, MERGE_RISK_UNVERIFIED, PROBATION, LOW_ANATOMY_MASS). Identity can be supported while COM is INELIGIBLE (`identity_track_continues` = true, status UNRESOLVED with the COM reason). Missing landmarks are never fabricated.
9. **Status**: MEASURED needs independent identity support with tier HIGH, anatomy tier INDEPENDENT, visible mass >= 0.85 with trunk. Otherwise PARTIAL with `status_limiting_factors` among LOW_MASS, IDENTITY_TIER_ACCEPT, IDENTITY_TEMPORAL_ONLY, ANATOMY_TEMPORAL_ONLY, ANATOMY_GEOMETRY_ONLY. PREDICTED/UNRESOLVED as before.
10. **Prediction** (unchanged safety rules, now logged): only from a MEASURED anchor of the same wrestler, <= 6 frames, no taint/ambiguity, inside the image and within one anchor-box diagonal; never refreshes identity or continuity; `prediction_log_v04.csv` records every considered/terminated prediction and a heuristic uncertainty radius (anchor-box diagonal x (0.1 + 0.1 k)), not a statistical interval.

## 2. Outputs
`tracking_v04.csv` (long format, `target` = A/B; separate identity / anatomy / COM / prediction fields; no single confidence number), `tracking_v04_landmarks_raw.csv`, `candidates_v04.csv` (every gated candidate with all evidence components), `identity_events_v04.csv`, `prediction_log_v04.csv`, `cuts_v04.json`, `annotated_v04.mp4`, `run_meta_v04.json`, `comparison_metrics_v04.json`, `regression_cases_v04.csv`.

## 3. What did not change
COM definition and mass thresholds (`com_model.py` byte-identical to V0.1), camera-event classifier (`cuts_events.py` byte-identical to V0.2/V0.3), pose-detection stage and cached detections, 10 manual seeds (identical to V0.3), geometric limb checks, prediction constants.

## 4. Known limitations (stated before the run)
* The embedding is a general image embedding, not a person-ReID model; its separation of the wrestlers is weak (seed leave-one-out: BOX 9/10, UPPER 8/10, LOWER 6/9) and is tested on the same 10 crops that define it.
* Reliabilities and scales are derived from only 10 seed crops.
* PART_IDENTITY_CONFLICT can only catch mixed-person skeletons whose upper and lower crops are embedded differently enough; it cannot see mixing inside one part, nor a merged detection whose parts all look like the assigned identity.
* There is still no independent ground truth. Every verdict in the audit is the developer's.
