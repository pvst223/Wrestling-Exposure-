## 7. Questions asked
* **Was coverage recovered through better evidence rather than weaker thresholds?** Partly. The 35 recovered emissions and 36 embedding-dependent frames came through a new evidence family (not a loosened gate), and V0.3's overlap relaxation was reversed. But total emission is lower than V0.3 and overlap-but-correct retention fell to 0.
* **Detectable contamination classes:** frames where the skeleton's upper and lower parts look like different people (PART_IDENTITY_CONFLICT), overlap with no independent evidence (rejected), A/B collapse, geometry/shared-joint conflicts.
* **Fundamentally ambiguous with this evidence:** a single merged detection whose parts all resemble the target, notably Cena carrying B (about f925–957 and similar scenes), which is still emitted and often MEASURED. Appearance coherence cannot see it.
