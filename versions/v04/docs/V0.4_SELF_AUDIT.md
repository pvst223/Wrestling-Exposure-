# V0.4 critical self-audit (developer; in-sample; single reviewer; not independent)

**What improved.** Four closeups V0.3 left blank (f692/725/732/736) are identity-supported with PARTIAL COM; 35 more V0.3-insufficient frames are emitted, none in known-bad. Known-bad emissions 44 → 27 (V0.2: 33). Viewed multi-body emitted 4 → 2 of 16; wrong-person emitted 2 → 1 of 18. A's identity never sits on V0.1's wrong box. Evidence is decomposed and logged per candidate; identity and COM eligibility are separate; MEASURED is much stricter (188 vs 351). Cuts, COM model and prediction rules are unchanged; 0 off-image predictions.

**What failed.** (1) Cena carrying B (about f925–957 and similar) is still emitted, often MEASURED: of 27 emissions in known-bad, my review judged 15 contaminated, 11 unsure, 1 clean. (2) Clean frames are wrongly rejected: 6 of 9 sampled V0.1-tracked rejections were clean Cena, mostly bent, crawling or airborne. Overlap-but-correct retention fell to 0. (3) REACQUISITION_UNCERTAIN removes 82 A frames because tier HIGH is unreachable in closeups. (4) Total emission fell (407 → 359). (5) Temporal continuation never produced an emission, so that machinery is untested here.

**What surprised me.** Most previously contaminated frames are now removed by the old overlap/UNVERIFIED rule, not by new detection ability. The embedding separated the wrestlers better than its weak seed statistics suggested, but that is judged on 10 seed crops plus my own eyes.

**Known unsafe outputs.** Any MEASURED/PARTIAL A frame near a carry or pile (e.g. f935, 950, 951, 957, 1082–1093) can hold landmarks from two bodies. f270 (referee merged) is PARTIAL. 6 of 21 contamination regression frames are still emitted. B has no ground truth; 9 sampled B emissions looked right, which is not a rate.

**Remaining missingness.** 1,133 of 1,501 A frames UNRESOLVED (IDENTITY_LOW_SCORE 275, CONTRA 224, LOW_ANATOMY_MASS 158, OVERLAP_UNVERIFIED 113, ANATOMY_FAIL 110, NO_CANDIDATE 102, REACQ_UNCERTAIN 82). 9 of 21 shots have zero A emission.

**Did the new identity evidence contribute beyond the thigh cue?** Yes mechanically: 36 emitted A frames needed it (colour reliability under the gate), none known-bad, none emitted by V0.3, all 12 sampled judged clean by me. But the model is a general CLIP-style embedding, not person-ReID, with reliabilities from 10 seed crops, so this is indicative only.

**Not holdout-ready because:** the embedding is not ReID and its reliability rests on 10 crops; merged-detection contamination is undetectable with these checks; the clean-frame rejection rate is high; every label and verdict is mine, non-blinded to design, on a clip used for design; thresholds are untested out of sample. V0.4 should be treated as a documented failure analysis plus a fail-closed prototype. V0.5 was not started.
