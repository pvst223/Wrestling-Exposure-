## 4. Definitions (identical to phase1_metrics.py v2)
- Independent unit: (clip, shot, truth_id), truth_id = named wrestler or REFEREE / STAFF / SPECTATOR / OTHER_WRESTLER_UNNAMED. All tracklets, frames and detections of that identity/class inside one shot collapse into ONE unit. They are descriptive counts only and never independent observations, for confidence intervals, tests or n.
- Detection decision: accepted-as-wrestler-X, rejected, or abstained; reject and abstain both count as not accepted.
- False accept (detection): accepted as any wrestler when the truth is not that wrestler (for REFEREE/STAFF/SPECTATOR/unnamed-other any acceptance is false). A/B swap = a wrestler detection accepted as the other wrestler.
- Unit-level FTA (primary, conservative): among negative units, the fraction having at least one false-accepted detection. Negative units are: wrestler units (as other-named-wrestler/swap negatives), referee, staff, spectator and unnamed-other-wrestler units. Reported by stratum and pooled. Exact Clopper-Pearson 95% CI over units. A stratum with fewer than 15 units is descriptive only (no inference).
- Coverage (primary): for each target (wrestler) unit, the fraction of that unit's detections accepted as its own identity; the unweighted mean over target units. CI: seeded percentile bootstrap over units (explicitly not exact); the secondary exact CI is for the binary 'target units with at least one correct accept'.
- Frame/detection-level FTA is secondary and descriptive; it has no CI and is never used in a decision.
- A/B swap units are counted inside FTA (other-named stratum) and reported separately.

## 5. Operating points (dev-only selection)
Per method a scalar acceptance threshold (baseline: tau in {0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70} and margin in {0.00, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10}; learned: softmax floor in {0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8}), chosen on dev only. Matched coverage: c* = the largest of {0.50, 0.40, 0.30, 0.20, 0.10} that both methods reach on dev, where coverage is the dev mean of per-unit accepted fractions. For each method choose on dev the setting with dev coverage >= c* and the lowest dev unit-level FTA (ties: highest coverage). Evaluate once on test. Secondary (exploratory): full test risk-coverage curves at unit level (phase1_metrics.risk_coverage).

## 6. Primary endpoint and decision rule (LOCO is NOT part of it)
Primary endpoint: paired exact one-sided sign test on the test negative units at the dev-selected operating points, with the dev-selected backbone. Per negative unit, false accept by baseline? by learned? b = units false-accepted only by the baseline, c = only by the learned head (phase1_metrics.paired_sign; each unit counts once however many tracklets or detections it has). Reasoning: with tens of independent units the test has little power; b=4,c=0 gives p=0.0625; 5,0 0.031; 3,0 0.125; 6,1 0.0625; 7,1 0.035 (verified in selftest5). The one-sided 0.10 level and the minimum of 4 discordant units let a clear consistent improvement be recognised without claiming a precise effect size.
KEEP (all must hold): (1) b + c >= 4, b > c, one-sided exact p <= 0.10; (2) learned test coverage (mean of unit fractions) >= baseline test coverage - 0.10; (3) no negative stratum with >= 15 units where the learned head has >= 3 more false-accept units than the baseline; (4) the annotation gate of section 2 is satisfied (otherwise the outcome is PROVISIONAL and capped at MODIFY).
KILL (all must hold): b <= c on test; the baseline had room to be beaten (at least 5 false-accept units on test); annotation gate satisfied.
INCONCLUSIVE-DATA: baseline has fewer than 5 false-accept units on test; fewer than 15 post-seed test target units; analysis cannot be completed (missing dependency, labels or annotation).
MODIFY: everything else, including improvement that needs a coverage loss > 0.10, mixed strata, insufficient evidence, or results explained by the disclosed data limits.
Interpretation guard: with 4 effective training identities and a shifted test domain, KILL means this formulation did not outperform on this pilot, not that learned re-ID is unpromising. KEEP means the evidence justifies scaling data and development, not that the method works.
