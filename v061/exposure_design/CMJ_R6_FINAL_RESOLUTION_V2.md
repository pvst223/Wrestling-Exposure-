## 3. What the chapter contains
- Abstract: history and characteristics of women's pro wrestling in Japan; based on interviews with 25 women pro wrestlers (abstract: 2004–2005) and numerous observed matches (introduction: matches observed mainly 2002–2005); physical transformation; fights based on mutual trust with two rules (absorb opponents' techniques; do not injure opponents).
- Methods statement: in-depth semi-structured interviews with 25 women wrestlers plus match observation; details deferred to Aiba (2017), a book.
- Sections: definition of pro wrestling as stage performance combining sport, acrobatics, dance and theatre; basic match style and rules; history of men's and women's pro wrestling in Japan including three "boom" periods; trainee-to-rookie physical transformation (basic conditioning exercises, break falls, rope work, sparring, professional test); the meaning of a "fight" (trust, punching and kicking, unwritten rules); pro wrestling as entertainment; conclusion on bodies, gender norms and presentation.
- Numbers present are descriptive/contextual only: wrestler count, years, match time limits as a rule description (30–60 min), a wrestler's anecdotal report of a professional test (50 knuckle push-ups), repetition counts of training drills as recalled by interviewees. None is a research measurement.
