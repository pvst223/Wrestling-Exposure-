# R3 standard v2 (reviewer: APPROVED WITH MODIFICATION, 2026-10-18)

This is a project governance/evidence standard, not a legal determination. It restates `R3_RESOLUTION_REPORT.md` section 2 with the reviewer's modifications; the report is preserved unchanged. R3 stays `UNRESOLVED / THIRD-PARTY TRANSMISSION BLOCKED` until the reviewer approves a source. Outreach is not R3 PASS.

## Preserved
- Q1: authorization to obtain and use the footage privately (copying, frame extraction, crop derivation, private storage). Q2: authorization to transmit the minimum necessary derived still crops to Google Gemini consumer web for the frozen annotation procedure. Recorded separately.
- Public availability or research purpose alone is not evidence of permission; the acquisition method must itself be permitted; ambiguous evidence = UNRESOLVED; per-source evidence kept privately and hashed; reviewer approval required before a source becomes Holdout #2-eligible.

## Modification 1 - counsel
Counsel review is recommended/escalatory, not mandatory for every source. A sufficiently explicit written authorization from a verified rights holder may satisfy the evidence gate without an independent counsel opinion if it clearly covers the actual Q1 and Q2 activities and no material conflicting restriction or unresolved rights issue is identified.

## Modification 2 - depicted persons
Do not assume that every referee/staff/spectator needs individual consent, or that a promotion automatically holds every personality/privacy right. Require the grantor to state factually, to the best of its authority: what rights it controls; whether its authorization covers use of images of participating performers/personnel for the proposed processing; what event notice/ticket/filming terms, if any, applied to attendees; and any known limitation or category it cannot authorize. A material unresolved issue exposed by these answers keeps the source UNRESOLVED and is escalated for additional permission or qualified legal review; no legal conclusion is drawn by the project.

## Routes
Route A (independent promotion/rightsholder written permission) is primary. Route C is fallback. Route B is deferred unless A cannot produce a sufficient pool. Route D/WWE is not prioritized. Route E (fair use) is never sufficient alone.

## Process per reply
Store the reply and any documents privately and hash them. Prepare a PROPOSED Q1 and Q2 determination (PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED, with reasons) on `R3_SOURCE_RECORD_TEMPLATE.json` for reviewer assessment. Neither Claude nor the owner self-declares R3 PASS.
