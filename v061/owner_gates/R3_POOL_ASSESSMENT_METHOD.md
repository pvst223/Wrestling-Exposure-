# Prospective pool assessment method (no footage is accessed)

Purpose: judge, from grantors' own statements and public-domain facts about their output, whether the permissions obtained could plausibly supply the frozen Holdout #2 minimums (spec_v4 02_DATA_PLAN 2.5): at least 4 clips, at least 8 named identities, at least 150 units, at least 60 target units, at least 60 negative units with at least 15 units in each of at least two negative strata (for example referee and staff/spectator), from at least 2 distinct events and at least 2 sources. A unit = (clip, shot, identity/class). Disjointness (spec 2.6) also applies: no development identity as a named wrestler, and no clip from the same event, broadcast, source file or upload as any development clip.

## Inputs taken only from grantors' statements (the template asks for them)
Number of events/shows; number of separate matches; named performers; whether produced with several camera angles. The custodian (CUSTODIAN-01) checks the performer NAMES against the development identity list (Orton, Rollins, HBK, Benjamin, Rock, HHH, Lesnar, Bryan, Andrade, Mysterio, Omega, Cena, Jeff Hardy) from names only, never imagery, and records pass/fail with counts.

## Checks (tick per source; all counts are planning estimates, not tuned and not model-informed)
1. Identities: at least 8 named, none in the development list, across the permitted sources.
2. Events/sources: at least 2 events and at least 2 sources among sources with Q1 and Q2 both PASS (a source that passes only Q1 cannot supply Gemini-annotated units).
3. Clips: at least 4 clips; each Holdout clip should plausibly feature two named performers in at least 4 distinct shots, which is more likely from multi-camera productions than from single static cameras (a factual question to ask, not a screen on footage).
4. Units: at least 150 units total, 60 target, 60 negative, two negative strata at 15 or more. Units scale with number of shots and the number of distinct people (performers, referee, staff, spectators) visible per shot; a calibration for units per clip can be taken from the frozen development counts (no performance data) before outreach results are known.
5. Minimisation: only the reserve (20%) goes to Gemini; the rest of Holdout #2 is not transmitted.

## Output
A one-page prospective feasibility note per set of replies: for each minimum, "plausibly met / plausibly not met / unknown" with the grantor statement it rests on. It is not selection of footage, not an inspection, and not a performance-based choice. It is reviewed by the reviewer with the per-source records.
