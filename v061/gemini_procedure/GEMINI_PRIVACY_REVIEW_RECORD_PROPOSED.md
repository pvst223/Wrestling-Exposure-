# Gemini consumer-app route: privacy / retention / data-use review record (PROPOSED; owner to verify, capture and hash)

Status: proposed record for reviewer inspection. Not frozen. Nothing has been transmitted to Gemini. Claude is not a lawyer and did not verify Google's terms beyond the page read below.

## 1. Route
Consumer Gemini web application (Gemini Apps), with Keep Activity OFF and fresh/temporary chats. This route is not assumed to have the same data handling as the API or an enterprise/Workspace route.

## 2. What Google's current Gemini Apps documentation says (as read 2026-10-18; page "last updated October 13, 2026" per the page; read through a summarising fetch, so the owner must capture and hash the page itself)
Source page: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961
- Chats with Keep Activity off, and temporary chats, are retained with the account for 72 hours and used to respond to the user and to protect Google, its users and the public.
- Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models. With Keep Activity off and no feedback submitted, Google states it does not use future chats to improve its AI models.
- If feedback is submitted, the associated data is collected and used to maintain and improve Gemini Apps, and reviewed feedback may be retained up to 3 years, disconnected from the account. Feedback must therefore never be submitted on annotation conversations.
- The page states that even with Keep Activity off or temporary chats, Google still uses chats to respond, including with help from human reviewers.
- With Keep Activity off, other Connected Apps (such as Google Workspace) are unavailable.

## 3. Mandatory statement for the record
According to Google's current Gemini Apps documentation, chats with Keep Activity off are not used to train Google's AI models unless feedback is submitted, but are retained for up to 72 hours for service and safety purposes. This route CANNOT be represented as zero-retention, and CANNOT be represented as guaranteed free of human safety review.

## 4. Conditions of use
Keep Activity OFF verified before any study material is sent; fresh/temporary chat per item with no prior study context; no feedback of any kind; unnecessary Connected Apps disconnected; Search/tools not intentionally invoked; only the minimum frozen material (two reference images, one item image, the frozen prompt) is sent, reserve items only; nothing posted or shared; no footage, frames or crops on GitHub; conversations deleted after copying the response where the UI allows (deletion does not shorten the stated 72-hour retention).

## 5. Third-party transmission is gated by R3
The images are footage-derived and show spectators and staff. Sending them to a third-party service must be separately established as permissible under R3 (source rights, licence or platform terms) for each source BEFORE any transmission. R3 is OPEN and each source is UNRESOLVED. Sources not so established are not sent. This privacy record does not itself authorize transmission.

## 6. Limits to disclose in results
Model version, temperature and other generation settings are not controllable or exposed on this route; the UI model designation may change over time; outputs may not be exactly reproducible. These are recorded per item and reported as limitations.
