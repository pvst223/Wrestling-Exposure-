## 1. Sample (reviewer ruling: Option B, ±15)
- Frames reviewed in M9 V1 and M9B (120) are absolutely excluded, and so is every frame within **±15 source frames** of one in the same clip (inclusive: |Δ|≤15 excluded).
- **RELEASED** stratum: target **60 unique frames** from frozen rows that the readiness machinery releases (recomputed from frozen caches, parity-verified). **Controls:** 30 `CONTROL_ASSIGNED` (ASSIGNED_A/B, measured) and 30 `CONTROL_HARDVETO` (identity ACCEPT, measured, ownership-withheld with ≥1 non-Tier-A reason, either state). Same exclusions; a frame appears in at most one stratum.
- One row per (clip, frame), seeded choice. Seed = int("45bd7c69",16) mod 2^31 (prefix of the frozen M9B verdict hash).
- **Spacing ladder** within each clip, strictest first: ≥30 frames, then ≥15, then ≥0 (distinct frames). A level is relaxed only when no admissible candidate remains at the stricter level in any clip and the stratum target is still unmet. Picks go to the clip with the fewest picks so far that still has an admissible candidate; shots are visited round-robin in seeded order. This preserves clip/shot stratification as far as feasible; any unavoidable clip imbalance, shortfall, picks per spacing level, adjacent-gap histogram and eligible-frame counts are written to `M9C_SAMPLING_LOG.json` and registered in the ledger BEFORE any image is viewed.
- Sizes are not adjusted if the target cannot be reached; a shortfall is reported, not hidden. Planning counts (frozen readiness): 89 unique released frames survive ±15, so the 60 target is reachable only with relaxation to ≥15 (max 51 at ≥30, max 60 at ≥15).

## 2. Blind review
- Primary reviewer: Claude (designated, non-independent). Images only; the hidden key, sheet and sampling log are not opened. Items are shuffled across all three strata (`M000…M119`). Released items show **only** the released landmarks and bones with both endpoints released, in the same neutral solid style as controls' skeletons (unmodified M9 layout; no "PARTIAL" tag, no distinct colour). Stratum can still be partly inferable from the number of drawn landmarks; disclosed.
- Verdicts: CORRECT / PARTIAL_BUT_USABLE / CONTAMINATED / NOT_JUDGEABLE, applied to what is actually drawn (definitions of M9_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_V1). One extra pre-registered field per item: `contact` = YES if two or more bodies visibly touch/overlap in or next to the drawn person's box, else NO, judged from the image alone.
- Verdicts frozen as JSON {id: {verdict, contact, note}}, hashed, ledgered (M9C1), committed and pushed BEFORE the key is opened.

## 3. Second reviewer (independent; project owner if available)
- ≥30 released frames (30 chosen proportionally by clip, seeded) plus 10 ASSIGNED and 10 hard-veto controls, 50 items in total, under **separate shuffled ids (`O000…O049`)** from the same blinded images. The owner sees no Claude verdict, no stratum/class, no ownership state/reasons, no identity state or scores, no merge-guard state, no key, and no sheet. The owner records the same four verdicts (contact optional).
- Both reviewers' verdict files are frozen, hashed and ledgered before either is unblinded or compared. If the owner is unavailable, the run is labelled single-reviewer.
- Claude's verdicts alone decide the pre-registered KEEP/MODIFY/KILL. The owner's review is an independent reproducibility/sensitivity analysis reported separately.

## 5. Open interpretation items for the reviewer (not decided here; reported, never applied automatically)
1. **Per-landmark KILL clause vs KEEP threshold.** The approved clause "OWN_ONLY landmark on the wrong body in ≥2 distinct shots → KILL" is true of every contaminated released frame by construction (a contaminated released frame is one whose drawn, OWN_ONLY landmark lies on another body). Read literally, two contaminated frames in two shots would KILL even at 2/60 = 3.3%, whereas KEEP permits up to 5.0%. The join reports the literal trigger separately and applies the proportion rules only. Needs a ruling before execution.
2. **Hard-veto control vacuity.** If the hard-veto control shows 0 contamination, "≥2× released" is satisfied vacuously; the join flags this as uninformative.
3. **Gate not satisfied but no MODIFY/KILL trigger** (e.g. <50 judgeable, control ratio failing) yields `NOT_KEEP_CRITERION_UNMET_NEEDS_REVIEWER_INTERPRETATION`, not an automatic MODIFY.
