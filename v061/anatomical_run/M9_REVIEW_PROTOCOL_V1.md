# M9 BLIND REVIEW PROTOCOL V1 (frozen before the first verdict)
Authorized by reviewer ruling "ANATOMICAL INTEGRATION EXECUTION V1" (accept development-only; M9 review only; no resampling; no rule changes). Status: ANNOTATION-PROVISIONAL, single reviewer, exploratory; NOT validated anatomical accuracy.
**Reviewer = Claude (the assistant that implemented the pipeline).** This is NOT an independent reviewer; knowledge of the system design is a disclosed bias source. Blinding is procedural: the reviewer never reads the hidden key, row status, ownership state/reason, identity code/score, or merge-guard state during review.
**Sample:** the frozen 60-frame sheet `M9_REVIEW_SHEET_BLIND.csv` (sha256 recorded in AR1/result). No resampling.
**Review images:** built only from (clip, frame, box) in the sheet + the raw frozen pose detections + the source video. The reviewed detection (matched by box IoU) is drawn with a cyan box and its COCO-17 skeleton (same display rule for every item: keypoint conf >= 0.7, bone length <= 0.75 of box diagonal); other pose detections are thin grey boxes only; a zoomed crop is shown beside the full frame. No status panel, no labels other than the random review id.
**Verdicts (exactly one per item), frozen definitions:**
- CORRECT: visible emitted landmarks/skeleton correspond to the reviewed person's anatomy without material cross-person contamination.
- PARTIAL_BUT_USABLE: incomplete/occluded anatomy, but visible landmarks correspond to the reviewed person sufficiently to represent that person's pose.
- CONTAMINATED: material landmarks/segments belong to another person or create a materially incorrect body representation.
- NOT_JUDGEABLE: source imagery/occlusion prevents a defensible judgment.
Judgments concern whether the DRAWN skeleton belongs to the localized person; they say nothing about wrestler identity. A short free-text note per item is allowed (anatomical observation only; must not guess system status).
**Freezing:** all 60 verdicts are written to a draft file; once complete they are hashed, registered in the ledger and committed BEFORE the hidden key is read. The key is joined exactly once by `m9_join.py` (registered/hashed before review). No verdict may be edited after freezing.
**Analysis (descriptive; exact Clopper-Pearson 95% CIs):** (1) verdict counts overall; (2) frozen ASSIGNED stratum; (3) frozen WITHHELD stratum; (4) withheld verdicts by ownership state/reason; (5) every CONTAMINATED ASSIGNED frame individually; (6) share of withheld frames judged CORRECT or PARTIAL_BUT_USABLE; (7) share of assigned frames judged CONTAMINATED. Proportions use judgeable items (excluding NOT_JUDGEABLE) as denominator; NOT_JUDGEABLE counts are reported alongside. n is small (30 per stratum, frames clustered within shots): intervals are wide and carry no population claim.
