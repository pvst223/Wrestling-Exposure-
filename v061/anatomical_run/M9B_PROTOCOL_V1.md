# M9B TARGETED BLIND DIAGNOSTIC - PROTOCOL V1 (frozen before any image is generated or viewed)
Authorized by reviewer ruling "M9 V1" (exploratory; no anatomical rule/threshold change; no rerun of identity, pose, segmentation, ownership or anatomy). Single reviewer = Claude (non-independent), ANNOTATION-PROVISIONAL, exploratory, NOT validated. Merge guard diagnostic-only; M7 open/non-blocking.
**Population (exact):** frozen Anatomical Integration V1 records with `pose_status = MEASURED` AND `identity in {ACCEPT_A, ACCEPT_B}` AND `ownership in {OWNERSHIP_UNCERTAIN, MIXED_BODY_SUSPECTED}`, excluding the 60 (clip, frame) items already used in M9 V1 (so no remembered verdicts). Population size 1383 rows (UNCERTAIN 1031, MIXED 352); 1173 unique (clip, frame).
**Deterministic sampling rule (60 unique frames):** seed = int("fb302b96", 16) % 2**31 (prefix of the M9 V1 frozen-verdict hash). Allocation: 15 per clip = 7 MIXED_BODY_SUSPECTED + 8 OWNERSHIP_UNCERTAIN per clip (MIXED is deliberately oversampled so both states are analyzable; population-weighted pooled estimates are also reported). Within each clip x state cell: shots are visited round-robin in a seeded random order; inside a shot a seeded-random candidate row is drawn; one row per (clip, frame) (unique frames); candidates must be >= 30 source frames from every frame already selected in the same clip (both states) to limit temporal clustering; if a cell cannot be filled the gap relaxes to 15, then 0 (any relaxation is logged). Review ids are assigned by a seeded shuffle.
**Review images / blinding:** built by the unchanged `m9_review_pack.py` from (clip, frame, box) only: cyan box + its COCO-17 skeleton (conf >= 0.7), other detections as grey boxes, zoomed crop, random review id. Hidden from the reviewer: ownership state/reasons, A/B status and identity, confidence/q, merge-guard state, the key. The key (private) is joined exactly once after all 60 verdicts are frozen and hashed.
**Verdict definitions (unchanged from M9 V1):** CORRECT / PARTIAL_BUT_USABLE / CONTAMINATED / NOT_JUDGEABLE, as defined in `M9_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_V1.md`. No tuning or rule change on the basis of intermediate results.
**Reporting (descriptive; exact Clopper-Pearson 95% CIs):** overall counts; counts by UNCERTAIN vs MIXED; by ownership reason (per reason; items may carry several reasons); by clip; share CORRECT+PARTIAL among judgeable (excl. NOT_JUDGEABLE) with CI (unweighted, and population-weighted by cell with a normal-approximation-free descriptive weighted proportion only, no CI); contamination share with exact CI; every contaminated case individually; veto/reason families associated with usable withheld anatomy. Prospective reason families: **mask_overlap_ownership** {OPPONENT_LANDMARKS_IN_MASK, CONTESTED_LANDMARKS, TOO_FEW_OWN_ONLY_LANDMARKS, MASK_NOT_EXPLAINED_BY_SKELETON}; **mask_span_other_instance** {BONE_SPANS_INSTANCES, LANDMARKS_IN_OTHER_INSTANCE, SOME_LANDMARK_IN_OTHER_INSTANCE}; **mask_support_missing** {NO_OWNER_MASK, FEW_LANDMARKS, BONES_LEAVE_MASK}; **geometry_cross_body** {LIMB_TOO_LONG:*, TORSO_SIDES_ASYMMETRIC, SHARED_JOINT_CONFLICT, TEMPORAL_JOINT_JUMP}; **plausibility_other** {anything else, e.g. LANDMARK_OUTSIDE_OWN_BOX, TEMPORAL_SCALE_JUMP}. An item counts in every family it carries a reason for.
**Limits:** n=60 (clip x state cells of 7-8), frames cluster within shots, single non-independent reviewer; findings are diagnostic hypotheses, not evidence that any rule is wrong or right.
